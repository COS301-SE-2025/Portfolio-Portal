## 1. Landing Page

The **Landing Page** is the first screen you’ll see when visiting PortfolioPortal. It provides a quick overview of the platform's features and invites new users to get started.

- Scroll down to learn more about what PortfolioPortal offers.
- Click **“Get Started”** to begin the signup process.

Please refer to our [Google Docs document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1xpYEr_gpnPbiTEHjSDPLsoRo8oy0msK5yuGsxLp-_v0/edit?tab=t.0) for all images.

---

## 2. Sign Up Page

After selecting **“Get Started”**, you'll be directed to the **Sign Up** page.

1. Fill in your **name**, **email**, and **password**. You will need to confirm your password.
2. Click **“Sign up”** to create your account.
3. Already have an account? Click **“Sign In”** at the bottom of the page.

---

## 3. Sign In Page

The **Sign In Page** allows returning users to access their accounts.

- Enter your registered **email** and **password**.
- Click **“Sign in”** to proceed.
- If you forgot your password, click the **“Forgot Password?”** link for recovery options.  
  _(still yet to be implemented)_
- If this is your first time using PortfolioPortal, select the  
  **“Don't have an account? Sign Up”** link below the ‘Sign in’ button.

## 4. Home Page

Once signed in, you’ll be directed to the **Home Page** (4.1), which acts as the main dashboard.

### Features:

- A top **navigation bar** with links to the following:

  - **How It Works** (4.2) – a step-by-step guide
  - **Upload CV** (4.3) – upload your resume for OCR scanning
  - **Templates** (4.4) – preview the available 3D environments
  - **About** (4.5) – learn more about the team and project

- Alternatively, you can **scroll** through each section vertically.
- For more extensive help, click the **“?”** button at the bottom right of the screen.  
  This opens up the **help menu** (4.6).
- Switch between **dark** and **light mode** using the button in the top right of the screen.

## 5. Profile Page

Access your **Profile Page** via the navigation bar or profile icon.

Here you can:

- View and update **account details**
- **Store CVs** for easy access
- View a list of your **generated 3D portfolio sites**
- Download or delete old submissions

## 6. Your 3D-Generated Portfolio Site

Once your CV is uploaded and processed, you’ll be directed to your own, personalised **3D portfolio website**.

📸 _[Insert screenshot of a sample 3D portfolio here]_

### What to Expect:

- The content of your CV is presented **dynamically** in a **3D environment**.
- You’ll be randomly or algorithmically assigned a **template** (Space, Office, Forest, or Cave).
- Each section of your CV (e.g., Education, Experience, Skills) is displayed interactively — explore the scene to discover your achievements.

### Navigation Tips:

- Use your **mouse or keyboard** to explore the space.
- Hover over objects to reveal more information.
- Each scene is responsive and can be viewed on both desktop and mobile.  
  _(mobile viewing is still yet to be accounted for)_
